2027 Ford Explorer Trims: What Changes as You Move Up the Lineup?

The 2027 Ford Explorer lineup gives SUV shoppers five distinct choices: Active, ST-Line, Tremor, ST and Platinum. Rather than simply adding equipment at every step, Ford uses each model to emphasize a different type of driving: everyday versatility, sporty styling, off-road capability, performance or premium comfort.

This guide takes a trim-by-trim approach. Each section explains what you get at the trim level and the changes from the previous Explorer model, making it easier to understand whether moving up the lineup gives you the features you actually want.

Ford lists the 2.3L EcoBoost I-4 as standard on every Explorer model except ST, while the 3.0L twin-turbo EcoBoost V6 is available on Tremor and Platinum and standard on ST. The 2027 Explorer offers available Intelligent 4WD, seating for up to seven, up to 400 horsepower and a maximum towing capacity of 5,000 pounds when properly equipped.

2027 Ford Explorer Active

The Explorer Active establishes the foundation for the 2027 lineup. It is designed for shoppers who want a practical three-row SUV with modern connectivity, driver-assistance technology and everyday comfort without moving into the more specialized Explorer models.

Included on Active

  • 2.3L EcoBoost I-4 engine
  • 13.2-inch center display and Ford Digital Experience
  • Wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto compatibility
  • Heated front-row seats and a 10-way power driver's seat
  • Ford Co-Pilot360 driver-assistance features, including Adaptive Cruise Control
  • Power liftgate, LED headlamps and taillamps, and black roo-rack side rails

Best for: families and daily drivers looking for a well-rounded SUV.

2027 Ford Explorer ST-Line

The Explorer ST-Line keeps the 2.3L EcoBoost engine but adds a noticeably sportier personality. This is the Explorer for shoppers who want aggressive styling and additional technology without stepping into the performance-focused ST.

Included on ST-Line

  • Includes all features from the Active 200A and Active Comfort package
  • 20-inch Ebony Black-painted machined aluminum wheels replace the Active's standard 18-inch Sparkle Silver wheels
  • A 360-degree camera is added to the driver-assistance technology package
  • Unique cloth-trimmed front- and second-row seats featuring red stitiching
  • B&O Sound System by Bang & Olufsen with 10 speakers and a subwoofer is included
  • A heated leather-wrapped steering wheel is included, with heated second-row seats available
  • Available BlueCruise hands-free highway driving

Best for: shoppers who want a sportier-looking Explorer with more technology and audio equipment while retaining the 2.3L EcoBoost.

2027 Ford Explorer Tremor

The Explorer Tremor changes the mission of the Explorer. Instead of focusing primarily on appearance, it adds factory-engineered hardware for drivers who want to take their three-row SUV beyond ordinary pavement.

Included on Tremor

  • Includes all features from the Active 200A and Active Comfort package
  • 18-inch high-gloss Ebony Black-painted wheels with Electric Spice accents and all-terrain tires replace the ST-Line's 20-inch street-oriented wheels
  • Selectable Off-Road drive mode is added for more specialized terrain
  • An available 3.0L twin-turbo EcoBoost V6 joins the powertrain choices
  • A model-exclusive Torsen limited-slip rear axle supports the Tremor's off-road mission
  • Off-road-tuned suspension increases ride height to 8.7 inches of minimum running ground clearance
  • Front and rear underbody deflector plates provide additional protection
  • Electric Spice-painted tow hooks, Tremor graphics and off-road auxilliary lights distinguish the exterior
  • Available 980-watt B&O audio with 14 speakers, heated second-row seats and Tremor-logo seating add comfort

Best for: drivers who want a 2027 Ford Explorer built with off-road capability as a central priority. Ford lists Tremor with Intelligent 4WD as standard.

2027 Ford Explorer ST

The Explorer ST takes the lineup in a completely different direction from Tremor. Where Tremor is engineered around off-road driving, ST is created for on-road performance, with the 3.0L EcoBoost V6, sport-tuned suspension and performance braking hardware.

Included on ST

  • Includes all features from the ST-Line 300A
  • The 3.0L twin-turbo EcoBoost V6 becomes standard, producing up to 400 horsepower and 415-lb-ft of torque
  • Sport-tuned suspension replaces the Tremor's off-road-tuned suspension
  • 21-inch Magnetite-painted aluminum wheels replace the Tremor's 18-inch all-terrain setup
  • Performance brakes with red-painted calipers are added
  • Quad-tipped exhaust and ST-specific exterior details emphasize performance
  • BlueCruise hands-free highway driving is equipped, along with PowerFold thrid-row seats
  • Heated and ventilated front-row seats, red accent stitching and ambient lighting add cabin upgrades
  • The available new ST Sinister Package adds 21-inch Gloss Black-painted wheels, unique accent lighting and black-outlined ST badges

Best for: drivers who want the most performance-oriented Explorer experience and prioritize power, suspension tuning and braking performance.

2027 Ford Explorer Platinum

The Explorer Platinum is the premium-focused alternative to the performance-oriented ST. It retains the ability to deliver strong power with its available 3.0L EcoBoost V6, but its major emphasis is luxury, convenience and upscale interior equipment.

Included on Platinum

  • Includes all features from the Active 200A and Active Comfort package
  • 20-inch Luster Nickel-painted aluminum wheels replace the ST's 21-inch Magnetite wheels
  • Satin aluminum exterior accents, adaptive headlamps and an Open On Approach liftgate emphasize premium convenience
  • Available 3.0L EcoBoost V6 power remains available, but Platinum prioritizes comfort rather than ST-specific performance hardware
  • Heated, ventilated and massageing front-row seats with leather inserts add a major interior comfort upgrade
  • PowerFold third-row seats are included
  • BlueCruise hands-free highway driving is equipped with a 1-year-plus-90-day plan included
  • An available panoramic Vista Roof with power shade opens the cabing to more light
  • 980-watt B&O Sound System with 14 speakers, heated second-row seats, available premium leather and ambient lighting reinforcce the luxury focus

Best for: shoppers who want a premium three-row SUV with advanced comfort and technology rather than the ST's performance-first hardware.
